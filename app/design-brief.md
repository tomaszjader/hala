# HALA — design brief

A static landing page about discovering shepherding in the Polish Tatras, published on GitHub Pages.

- Mood: a welcoming sunny July morning, natural warm daylight, green pasture and believable sheep.
- Visual language: documentary-inspired photography, restrained motion, recognisable Podhale clothing and honest textures. These are AI visualisations, not records of specific people or places.
- Palette: warm cream `#f7f2e7`, pine-olive `#334d3a` and `#304b38`, straw `#eddda8`, oat `#ede4c9`.
- Journey: landscape and shepherd opening, introduction to the work, portrait, closer film chapter, first steps and closing invitation.
- Hero and second film use the existing scroll-scrub behaviour. Preserve forward/reverse scrolling, mobile framing and exact first-frame posters.
- Reduced-motion preferences show static images. Keep copy readable over every scene and preserve the responsive composition.
- Four stills use GPT Image 2.6 XHigh 2K; both five-second films use Kling 4.0 at 1080p. The closing image is a separate vertical sunny pasture scene.
- The form prepares an email draft without collecting data. The page is a concept, not an official recruitment service.

Runtime media are limited to the files in `public/assets/`; model provenance is recorded in `media-manifest.json`.
